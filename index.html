Our Story

From one Idea, Expanding Outward

The “E” in E.Universe comes from founder Yusuke Hayashi’s first venture, Enjonius, a word created by combining Enjoy and Genius. At its core is a belief: there is genius in enjoyment. When people become deeply absorbed in something they truly love, talents that have long been dormant begin to awaken.
