Noor

A light within E.Universe.

Noor is where Islamic design is practised as design —
not as decoration, not as a category, but as a way of seeing.
Geometry that holds meaning. Restraint that carries weight.
Objects that are made to be kept.

The work here is led by designers, not by product lines.
What Noor makes changes with who is making it.
What stays the same is the standard: quiet, exact, and true to its source.

Noor is a branch of E.Universe.
One day it may be its own tree.
