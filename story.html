The Beginning

The First Spark

The story of E.Universe began with Enjonius, the founder’s first venture. Created from the words “Enjoy” and “Genius,” it was built on a simple idea: there is genius within the things we genuinely enjoy. Over time, that idea grew into a larger vision — one where individual talent and passion could reach beyond the self and expand outward, like a universe.

Year 2026

Where We Stand

Today, E.Universe is taking its first steps as a design and digital direction studio based in Nagoya. While the vision extends far into the future, the focus remains clear: helping people and businesses find direction, creating meaningful work, and building a place where effort is valued regardless of background, nationality, or circumstance.

Mission & Vision - E.Universe

Mission:

To make careful, honest work visible and recognised whatever the background it comes from.

Vision:

A place where people and businesses find their direction, and where effort is valued regardless of nationality or circumstance.

This requires patience, nuance, and a deep respect for the people and businesses we work alongside. We take the long view on everything.

Our Values

01

Sincerity

We say what we mean, and we mean what we say. No overpromising. No jargon. Just honest work and honest conversation — with every client, at every stage.

02

Substance

Flash fades. We build things that last — relationships, strategies, designs — because we take the long view on everything we do. The work should stand on its own.

03

Connection

We believe the best outcomes emerge from genuine relationships, not transactions. Every client we work with is a long-term partner in our eyes, from the first conversation.

E.Universe

E.Universe is a small Nagoya studio for design and digital direction, helping people and businesses move forward, where effort is recognised, whatever your background.
